# Circl'd Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-02_

## What Circl'd collects

- Your device's GPS location, used only to build a route from where you
  are and to track your progress during a run or walk.
- The display name you optionally add on the Profile screen.
- The activities you complete (distance, duration, pace, and location
  label).

## Where it's stored

All of it stays on your device, in local app storage. Circl'd has no
backend server and no user accounts, so nothing you enter is ever
transmitted to or stored by the developer. Deleting the app deletes this
data.

## Third parties involved in generating a route

Building a route sends only raw coordinates (never your name or any
other identifying information) to:

- **OpenRouteService** — to calculate the loop itself.
- **OpenStreetMap / Nominatim** — to turn your coordinates into a
  readable address.
- **data.police.uk** — where you're in England, Wales, or Northern
  Ireland, to fetch recent public crime reports near you.

None of these services receive your name, device identifiers, or
activity history.

## What Circl'd does not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites,
no selling or sharing of your data, no account sign-up.

## Your choices

You can delete individual activities from the Activity Log, clear your
profile name at any time, or remove all data at once by uninstalling the
app.
